# BSTRDZ — Identité éditoriale et manifeste (7.1)

Référence du discours BSTRDZ. Le manifeste est publié sur la page CANEM (`/about`) ; sa source
dans le code est `src/config/canem.ts`.

## Direction

BSTRDZ n'est plus uniquement un territoire textile. C'est un espace de création et
d'expérimentation, à la frontière du physique et du digital, où coexistent la matière, l'image,
le son, le mouvement, le code et le geste.

- Craft over convention.
- Experience over explanation.
- Intention over perfection.
- Exploration over categorization.
- Creation over consumption.

**Ton** : poétique, sensoriel, brut, légèrement mystique, intemporel.

**À éviter** : la présentation d'agence créative, l'argumentaire commercial, la liste de
compétences.

## Cotton Index

Concept éditorial officiel de BSTRDZ : l'**expression physique et textile** de la pratique
artistique. Ce n'est ni une marque indépendante, ni le sujet central du manifeste. Il n'a pas de
route, de catégorie ni d'entrée de navigation dédiées. SIMPLE et ses produits ne changent pas.

## Le manifeste

Deux colonnes défilent indépendamment, à des vitesses différentes. Leurs rencontres aléatoires
créent de nouvelles résonances. La structure est conceptuelle et n'est pas affichée : pas de titre,
de numérotation ni de CTA.

### THE TANGIBLE (colonne gauche)

The practice began as a way of looking, before it became a way of making. An instinct to observe, distort, assemble and leave a trace.

Everything begins with a gesture. A line drawn, a surface touched, a fragment collected. The medium comes later.

Ink, cotton, light and code belong to the same vocabulary. Different materials, different resistances, the same impulse.

Craft is not defined by the absence of technology. It is defined by the presence of intention.

Imperfection is not something to correct. Sometimes it is the only evidence that something has truly happened.

The Cotton Index is a physical extension of the practice. An evolving collection without seasons, where images inhabit fabric and ideas acquire weight.

Cotton carries time differently from a screen. It absorbs, fades, folds and remembers. The object continues its existence beyond the gesture that brought it into being.

Repetition is not a lack of ideas. It is a way of looking deeper into the same question.

Between intention and execution, something unexpected always finds its way in. The accident becomes part of the language.

Nothing is made to complete a collection. Each piece exists because, at some point, it needed to exist.

The archive grows at the pace of curiosity. Not according to a calendar, a market or the expectation of something new.

### THE INTANGIBLE (colonne droite)

An image can be heard. A sound can occupy space. A sequence can exist somewhere between memory and imagination.

The digital is not the opposite of the physical. It is another material, with its own textures, tensions and imperfections.

Code becomes a gesture. Motion becomes a language. Sound becomes an architecture without walls.

Some things are made to be understood. Others are made to be experienced.

There is no hierarchy between a drawing, a garment, a sound or a moving image. Each is a different manifestation of the same restless curiosity.

An experience begins where the object ends. Somewhere between what is revealed and what is felt.

Silence is part of the composition. Emptiness is part of the image. What remains unseen can carry as much weight as what is revealed.

The work moves between the familiar and the unknown, between order and accident, between the organic and the artificial.

Paris is a geographical condition, not a creative boundary. The work belongs wherever it finds a resonance.

Nothing here is entirely finished. Every creation carries the possibility of becoming something else, in another form, at another time.

The index does not close. It is a living record of gestures, experiments and encounters. An ongoing attempt to give form to the intangible.
