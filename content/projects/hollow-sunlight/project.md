We forge long hours for fleeting, stolen light,
We weave long weeks for a day’s brief flight,
We carve out days for hurried bread,
And stitch the night for dreams soon fled.

We build the year for scattered rest,
And chart our lives to a jester’s jest,
At journey’s end, we count our breath,
Life — a shadowed play with the whisper of death.

Bound to the ghost of gold, to the chains of the crowd,
Life is a flame — let us burn unbowed!
