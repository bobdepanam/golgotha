Beneath the clear skies of a gilded dawn,
We raced through fields where wonder shone,
Innocence our cloak, bright visions awake,
The world a tale for joy’s own sake.

But one breath of Time, and the magic flies,
A moment’s drift, and the dream-light dies,
The fragile ideal lingers, a gentle trace,
Childhood fades — yet hope keeps its place.
